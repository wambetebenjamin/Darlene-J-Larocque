Founder & Women's Life Coach

I understand what it means to rebuild a life

I am Darlene J. Larocque, founder of Master's Touch Women's Life Coaching. I am a Holy Spirit-led Women's Life Coach who helps women heal from painful experiences, rebuild their confidence and self-worth, and move forward with clarity and purpose.

I understand that trauma, rejection, abuse and unhealthy relationships can leave a woman questioning her value and losing sight of who she truly is. My desire is to provide a safe, compassionate and faith-centred space where women feel heard, supported and empowered.

I believe two of the greatest barriers to healing are an inaccurate view of God and an inaccurate view of ourselves. When those beliefs are renewed through Scripture, practical tools and compassionate support, lasting transformation becomes possible. And I do not simply teach what I have studied. I also know what it means to walk through adversity and discover that your past does not have the final word.

My mission

To partner with the Holy Spirit in the healing journey of women, providing faith-integrated coaching that restores identity, renews the mind, and empowers women to walk in wholeness, freedom and their God-given purpose.

I am the proud mother of three adult children, a cherished son-in-love and six wonderful grandchildren. I am also an ordained minister with a passion for preaching the transforming message of Jesus Christ, and I am available to speak in churches, prisons, women's shelters, retreats and women's conferences across Canada, the United States and internationally.
