Women's Breakfast Breakthrough Sessions

Hosted by Master's Touch Healing International Ministries. A welcoming, safe space for women to enjoy fellowship over light refreshments while growing in their relationship with God and experiencing His healing presence, through biblical teaching, practical application and meaningful discussion.

  • When: first Saturday of every month, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon
  • Next session: Saturday, October 3, 2026. Doors open at 9 a.m. sharp.
  • Where: 9 Innisfil Street, Barrie, Ontario
  • Cost: free. Registration helps us prepare refreshments and seating.
  • A little gift: the first three women to arrive early receive something special

Together we explore emotional healing, overcoming trauma and shame, identity in Christ, healthy relationships, forgiveness, spiritual growth and living with purpose.
