Meet Darlene

I understand what it means to rebuild a life.

I'm Darlene J. Larocque, founder of Master's Touch Women's Life Coaching. I'm a Holy Spirit-led Women's Life Coach who helps women heal from painful experiences, rebuild their confidence and self-worth, and move forward with clarity and purpose.

I understand that trauma, rejection, abuse and unhealthy relationships can leave a woman questioning her value and losing sight of who she truly is. My desire is to provide a safe, compassionate and faith-centred space where women feel heard, supported and empowered.

I believe two of the greatest barriers to healing are an inaccurate view of God and an inaccurate view of ourselves. When those beliefs are renewed through Scripture, practical tools and compassionate support, lasting transformation becomes possible. And I don't simply teach what I've studied; I also know what it means to walk through adversity and discover that your past does not have the final word.

What this concept changes

Fourteen decisions, each one aimed at a specific thing that's costing you clients today.

Your signature line leads The strongest sentence on your site is now the headline instead of a paragraph halfway down.
She sees herself in four seconds A "does this sound like you?" checklist turns a long paragraph into recognition.
Proof appears before the ask Credentials sit directly under the hero, above any price or button.
One obvious next step The main button is repeated in the header, the hero, the pricing section and the footer, and it always works.
A no-cost way in The free 15-minute call gives a hesitant woman somewhere safe to start.
"Most chosen" guidance Three paths are no longer equal: the middle one is recommended, which reduces decision paralysis.
Prices in CAD, stated plainly No ambiguity for a Canadian client, and per-session math is kept because it's honest and effective.
Your monthly event becomes a centrepiece Date, address, hours and free admission, with a register button instead of a buried paragraph.
A real contact destination Phone, email, hours, address and a form, all in one place, tappable on a phone.
Testimonials built in Three slots, positioned exactly where doubt appears. Marked as placeholders so nobody mistakes them for real quotes.
Group coaching is a page, not a Facebook link The announcement bar now leads somewhere with a waitlist, so interest can be captured instead of lost.
Your free guide earns the email "Your Identity in Christ" becomes a downloadable: the same content you already wrote, working every week.
Your book does more work Both a "buy" and a "invite me to speak" action from the same section.
Care, stated out loud A scope-of-practice and crisis note in the footer, protective for you, reassuring for her, and the most professional thing on the page.
