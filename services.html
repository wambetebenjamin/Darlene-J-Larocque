Is life coaching for you?

You may be in the right place if

You do not have to have everything figured out before beginning. Coaching is for women who are ready to move forward beyond what has held them back, rediscover their worth and identity, develop healthier relationships, and take intentional steps toward the life and purpose God has for them.

  • You have been through trauma, abuse, rejection or painful relationships, and you are ready to move forward.
  • You have lost confidence in yourself and want to rediscover your worth and identity.
  • You struggle with boundaries or unhealthy relationship patterns.
  • You know God has more for you, but you are unsure how to move forward.
  • You are ready to heal, grow and rediscover your God-given purpose.

“Sometimes the first step is simply deciding that you are ready for something to change.”

The signature journey: Heal · Rebuild · Rediscover

Six sessions, six steps

1 · Heal and become aware

Recognize experiences, patterns and beliefs that continue to influence your present.

2 · Rediscover your identity

Explore your worth, strengths, values and God-given identity.

3 · Rebuild confidence

Challenge limiting beliefs and develop a healthier, more confident sense of self.

4 · Establish healthy boundaries

Recognize your needs, communicate them appropriately and develop healthier relational patterns.

5 · Build healthier relationships

Explore reciprocity, communication, respect, emotional safety and healthy connection.

6 · Move forward with purpose

Bring everything together into a personal plan for continuing forward with confidence, faith and purpose.
